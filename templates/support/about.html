About Us

Dark Sky Games

Dark Sky Games is an independent game studio based in Eryri (Snowdonia) National Park, North Wales. We create thoughtful, atmospheric games inspired by place, story, and the natural world around us.

Working beneath one of the world's recognised International Dark Sky Reserves has shaped who we are and how we work. The vast night skies of Eryri influence our creative direction, our studio name, and our commitment to telling stories that encourage curiosity, reflection, and a sense of wonder.

We believe games can be more than escapism. At Dark Sky Games, we aim to craft experiences that spark imagination, and explore meaningful themes without losing sight of fun and immersion.

Eclipse Protocol is our debut science-fiction RPG, developed under these principles. While set among the stars, it reflects our real-world inspirations - exploration, responsibility, and the fragile beauty of the environments we inhabit.

As a small independent studio, we value creativity, sustainability, and building games with care. We're proud to create from North Wales and to share our work with players around the world.
