What can I buy with credits?
  • Energy Boosters: Temporary performance enhancements for your ship's systems
  • Pilot Customisation: Suits, helmets, and personal equipment
  • Ship Skins: Cosmetic designs to personalise your vessel
  • Weapon Skins: Unique visual themes for your arsenal
  • Emotes & Gestures: Express yourself in multiplayer
  • Ship Upgrades: Performance modifications and equipment
How are credits applied?

Important: You must be signed into your Eclipse Protocol account during purchase for credits to be applied. Credits are cross-platform, so they'll appear on any device where you're logged in with the same account.

Cross-Platform Support: Your credits work across PC (Steam/Epic), Xbox, Nintendo Switch, and PlayStation platforms when using the same account.

PC

Available as digital licence keys for:

  • Steam
  • Epic Games Store

Keys are delivered instantly via email after purchase.

Xbox

Available as digital licence keys for Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One.

Redeem through your Xbox account or Xbox app.

Nintendo Switch

Available as digital licence keys for Nintendo Switch.

Redeem through Nintendo eShop with your Nintendo account.

PlayStation

Purchase directly from PlayStation Store.

Due to PlayStation's digital distribution policies, we cannot sell PS5/PS4 keys directly.

Digital Game Purchases

We offer refunds within 14 days of purchase, provided:

  • You have played less than 2 hours of the game
  • The licence key has not been redeemed
  • You contact our support team with your order details
In-Game Credits

Credits purchases can be refunded within 7 days if:

  • The credits have not been spent in-game
  • You provide your purchase receipt
Note: Once a licence key is redeemed on any platform, it cannot be refunded. Please ensure compatibility before activation.

Do I need an Eclipse Protocol account?

Yes, an Eclipse Protocol account is required for:

  • Cross-platform progression and saves
  • Multiplayer matchmaking
  • Credits and in-game purchases
  • Community features and leaderboards
Cross-Platform Features

Supported across PC, Xbox, PlayStation, and Nintendo Switch:

  • Multiplayer - play with friends regardless of platform
  • Progression sync - your achievements and unlocks carry over
  • Credits - purchase once, use anywhere
  • Friends list - connect with players across all platforms
